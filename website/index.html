WHY IT EXISTS

The collection grew.
So did the pile of apps.

I had a bunch of readers lying around. Every reader meant another app, another workflow and another set of quirks.

Cartridge Studio started at that workbench. The goal is simple: give your collection a consistent home, wherever the cartridge came from and whichever supported reader you own.

For the retro gamer preserving an old favorite. The homebrew developer checking a new build. The DIYer, hardware hacker and curious person who wants to see what’s on the board.

* Implementation and physical qualification are distinct. INLretro GBA and manual NROM reads await hardware qualification. Spansion S29GL032M R4 erase/program is physically verified across all 4 MiB on GBxCart PCB 6 / L14. Other flash profiles retain their documented qualification limits; exact board and firmware matching is required.

See boards, firmware and qualification details ↗

Game Boy / Color

ROM-only, MBC1, MBC2, MBC3 and MBC5 ROMs. A supported mapper is a reading capability; it does not establish that a board is writable.

Game Boy Advance

Standard linear ROMs up to 32 MiB at 3.3 V. Two-pass verification, exact catalog matching and a manual ROM-size override.

NES & Famicom

Separate 72-pin and 60-pin slots. NROM-128/256 (mapper 0) and Broke Studio UNROM-512 (mapper 30). Other mappers are future work.

ROM operations only today. Saved games, RTC, SD-card flash cartridges and special banked GBA boards are not supported yet. No games are included.

BUILT TO KEEP THE BYTES RIGHT

A backup deserves
more than a progress bar.

01

Read twice. Compare.

Backup and verification compare two complete reads. Raw passes and an operation report preserve the evidence.

02

Identify by the data.

CRC32, SHA-1 and SHA-256 checksums. Known games match by full hash and size, with optional artwork after identification.

03

Make errors useful.

A wrong slot, unreadable header or unsupported board comes with a next step. Destructive actions require a matching flash profile and review.
